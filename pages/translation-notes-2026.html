紫色瓶盖黑枸杞水 · 中译英全文

2026-04-24

产品概念为全紫色瓶盖、粉液分离设计，旋盖时黑枸杞粉末自上而下注入水中，形成紫色花青素扩散轨迹。原文与参考译文如下。

English · 原文

A premium bottled water with a fully purple cap (replacing the original white cap), featuring a transparent bottle filled with clear water. The cap is a powder-liquid separation design. As the cap is twisted open, fine black goji berry powder stored inside the cap is released into the water from above. The powder disperses and creates stunning flowing purple anthocyanin trails, forming silky, thread-like diffusion patterns cascading downward from the top. The liquid shows elegant, smooth, ribbon-like purple gradients spreading in water, visually mesmerizing and delicate. Focus on the dynamic "top-down infusion" effect, with vivid purple streaks and fluid motion frozen mid-diffusion. High-speed capture aesthetic, ultra-detailed liquid dynamics, cinematic lighting, clean background, premium product photography style, hyper-realistic, 8K, sharp focus, studio lighting.

中文 · 参考译文

一款优质瓶装水，瓶盖为全紫色（替换了原本的白色瓶盖），透明瓶身内装满清澈的水。瓶盖采用粉液分离设计。当瓶盖被旋开时，储存于盖中的细磨黑枸杞粉末便会从上方释放，落入水中。粉末散开后，形成令人惊叹的流动紫色花青素轨迹，呈现出如丝般顺滑的线状扩散纹理，从顶部倾泻而下。液体中浮现出优雅、顺滑、缎带般的紫色渐变，在水中曼妙扩散，视觉效果精致而迷人。重点表现"自上而下注入"的动态效果，生动的紫色条纹与流动的姿态在半扩散的瞬间凝固。高速捕捉美学，超精细液体动力学，电影级布光，干净背景，高端产品摄影风格，超写实，8K，锐利对焦，影棚灯光。
