Twitter is an online social media and social networking service owned and operated by American company Twitter, Inc. The platform allows registered users to post or reply to texts, images, and videos known as tweets. Users can also like, retweet, and direct message other users, while unregistered users only have the ability to view public tweets.

Twitter has become a go-to platform for breaking news, entertainment, sports, and politics, with users able to get the full story with live commentary. Fans, players, commentators, and coaches come together on Twitter to root for what matters most - getting the win. The platform is also a popular place for gamers, with users able to find industry news, player feedback, and gamer-to-gamer tips.

To use Twitter's services, users must agree to the platform's Cookies Use policy, which includes using cookies for analytics, personalization, and ads. Third-party cookies are also placed and read on users' browsers for non-essential purposes, including targeting ads. Companies such as Google, LinkedIn, NewsCred, and Logicad collect personal data through these cookies for their own purposes.

Twitter also offers advertising opportunities for businesses, with Twitter Cards helping to richly represent content on the platform. Analytics are available to measure the effectiveness of advertising campaigns, and businesses can also create Lists to customize, organize, and prioritize the Tweets they see in their timeline.

However, it's worth noting that Twitter has recently faced criticism for inadvertently using personal information provided for safety or security purposes for advertising. The platform is addressing the issue through its Tailored Audiences and Partner Audiences advertising system.
