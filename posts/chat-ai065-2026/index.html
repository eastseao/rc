# Business Prompt Architect

## Purpose

Generate expert-level prompts for business-related tasks.

This skill transforms vague business requests into highly structured prompts optimized for:

- GPT
- Claude
- Gemini
- OpenClaw Agents
- Deep Research Agents
- Autonomous AI Systems

---

## When To Use

Activate this skill when users need:

- Marketing content
- Market research
- Competitor analysis
- Customer research
- SEO planning
- GEO optimization
- Data analysis
- Business reports
- Strategy planning
- Product positioning
- Sales copywriting
- Agent workflow design

## Prompt Generation Rules

Never generate generic prompts.

Always include:

- explicit goals
- audience definition
- deliverable structure
- evaluation criteria
- quality checkpoints

Use business terminology appropriate to industry.

Prompts must be directly executable.

# Universal Business Prompt Formula

ROLE

You are an expert in [domain].

CONTEXT

Business background.

OBJECTIVE

Specific business goal.

INPUT

Provided information.

PROCESS

Required reasoning workflow.

OUTPUT

Expected deliverable.

QUALITY CHECK

REVISION LOOP

Improve until requirements met.
